About Me

Research that puts people at the center

I'm a Senior UX Researcher with 8+ years driving research strategy for enterprise platforms, B2B tools, and AI-powered products at Cisco, Meta, NIH/NCI, and Schweitzer Engineering Labs. My work consistently centers on one goal: translating complex human needs into product decisions that create real adoption and impact.

Most recently at Cisco, I led AI-readiness research for the Approvals MCP initiative — one of Cisco's first ventures into agentic AI workflows. I defined the trust frameworks, automation thresholds, and mental models that now shape next-gen AI product direction for 80,000+ employees.

My path into UX is unconventional — and I think that's a strength. I spent 12 years in sales leadership, managing a $6M/month regional territory and a 25-person team. I know how decisions get made inside organizations, how to frame research for executives, and how to connect user insight to business outcomes.
