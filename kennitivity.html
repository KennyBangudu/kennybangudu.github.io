Enterprise Tech · AI Products
AI Agent Strategy

Defining the Trust Boundary for an Enterprise AI Agent

Survey n=405 IDIs n=11 Thematic Analysis

A large enterprise platform was planning to ship an AI agent to manage complex internal workflows for 6,800+ users across 3 global regions. Before building, they needed to know: would users trust it, and for what? A two-phase mixed-methods study (large-scale survey followed by in-depth scenario-based interviews) defined exactly where trust was high, where it dropped off, and what conditions users required before they would accept autonomous AI action.

Key Outcomes
  • → Established 3-tier AI trust framework adopted directly into product roadmap
  • → Defined 6 prioritized AI capabilities sequenced by user comfort, not engineering preference
  • → Prevented premature autonomous feature launch — saving estimated 6 months of rework
  • → Secured executive buy-in for phased AI rollout strategy grounded in user evidence
